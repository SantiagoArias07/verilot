Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026

VERILOT is an early-stage company building optical quality certification for recycled polymer flake. This notice explains, in plain English, what the information you send us through this site is used for.

What we collect

When you submit the deployment assessment or contact form, we collect only what you type into it:

  • Your name and work email address
  • Your company or facility, and its location
  • The polymer stream and approximate monthly tonnage you tell us about

We do not run advertising or third-party analytics trackers on this page, and we do not use cookies to profile you. Standard server logs (including IP address) may be processed by our hosting provider to deliver and secure the site.

Why we use it

Solely to respond to your enquiry — to scope a potential pilot, answer your questions and follow up about a deployment. That’s it.

Who sees it

Your submission is emailed to the VERILOT team through our email provider (Resend) and our hosting provider (Vercel). We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we don’t share it with anyone else except where required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only as long as needed to follow up and maintain our records. Ask us to delete your information at any time and we will.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you’ve sent, or to stop contacting you. Email us and we’ll take care of it.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@verilot.example.

This is a concise, good-faith notice for an early-stage product site, not legal advice. It will be expanded as VERILOT grows and formal data-processing agreements are put in place.
