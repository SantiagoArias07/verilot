How the Exchange works

Certify once. List, match and move automatically.

01CertifyA VERILOT module certifies the batch at the source: composition, confidence, tamper-evident hash.
02Auto-listEach closed certificate becomes a listing—polymer, grade, tonnage, price and location—no manual entry.
03MatchBuyers post specs. The engine hard-filters, then ranks by spec fit, landed cost, distance and confidence.
04MoveFreight is estimated per route (distance × rate × tonnage, trucks) so buyers compare landed cost, not sticker price.
