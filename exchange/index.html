How automated settlement will work Planned · v2
01Match & agree

Buyer accepts a ranked match; terms (tons, price, freight) lock into a draft deal.

02Escrow hold

Funds are held via Stripe Connect until the certified batch is dispatched.

03Move & verify

Freight is booked on the matched lane; delivery is checked against the certificate hash.

04Settle

On verified delivery, escrow releases to the seller minus the platform fee.
