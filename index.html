The problem

Recycling doesn’t fail for lack of material. It fails because no one trusts what they buy.

9%

of plastic waste worldwide is recycled. OECD, Global Plastics Outlook 2022.

A buyer receiving recycled flake can’t tell whether it carries PVC, metal, foreign caps or moisture until it’s processed—and one bad batch can damage an extruder or scrap an entire production run. Today, quality is checked by hand, too late, and to different standards at every plant.

01 The plant grinds, washes and bags the flake.
02 The batch ships with no reliable quality data.
03 The buyer inspects by hand or sends samples to a lab.
04 Price penalty, rejection or landfill.

What’s missing is trusted quality data—generated at the source and travelling with every batch.

How it works

One module on the outfeed belt. One certificate per batch.

Right before bagging, the system scans and weighs every gram. When the batch closes, it issues a certificate with estimated composition, evidence and an explicit confidence level.

01 Measure Vision and NIR spectroscopy read the stream continuously.
02 Analyze On-device AI identifies polymer type and contaminants.
03 Close the batch When the bulk bag fills, composition and grade are computed.
04 Certify A tamper-evident certificate with QR code and attached evidence.
05 Sell & report Share with buyers; data flows straight into ESG reporting.
